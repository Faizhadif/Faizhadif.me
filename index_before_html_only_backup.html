04. FAQ

Common Questions.

What is your typical process?
I start with a discovery phase to understand your objectives. Then, I move onto wireframing, high-fidelity UI design, and finally development. Throughout the process, I maintain clear communication to ensure we align with your vision.
How long does a project usually take?
A landing page redesign typically takes 2-3 weeks, while a full-scale web application can take anywhere from 1 to 3 months depending on complexity. Timelines are finalized during our discovery stage.
Do you work with startups and agencies?
Yes, I often integrate with existing agency teams as a senior technical resource or partner directly with startup founders to build MVPs from scratch.
